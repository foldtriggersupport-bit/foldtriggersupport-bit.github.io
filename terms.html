What FoldTrigger does

FoldTrigger turns the back (cover) screen of a foldable phone, volume keys, gamepad buttons and on-screen buttons into triggers. When you press a trigger, FoldTrigger sends the touch you set up to the app in front, such as a game. It uses Android's Accessibility service and an optional helper that you start yourself through Android's Developer options (Wireless debugging or USB debugging).
